## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-26

### Added
- YAML parser and writer — `yaml-read`/`yaml-read-string` and
  `yaml-write`/`yaml-write-string`, with `yaml-null`/`yaml-null?` and
  `yaml-ref`/`yaml-ref*` accessors
- Pure Scheme implementation, no C dependencies or build step
- CI workflow for automated testing
